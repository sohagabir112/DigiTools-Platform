# Chatbot knowledge (reference)

Training content for the AI assistant is maintained in code at [`src/data/chatKnowledge.js`](../src/data/chatKnowledge.js) and loaded from [`public/products.json`](../public/products.json).

To enable free LLM replies, follow [chatbot-setup.md](./chatbot-setup.md) and add `GROQ_API_KEY` or `GEMINI_API_KEY` on Vercel.
